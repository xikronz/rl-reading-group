Eighteen acronyms. One estimator.

The recent explosion of *PO methods is not eighteen unrelated algorithms. Almost every one of them is a patch to a small number of failure modes in the same estimator, the one descended from REINFORCE. This page pins each method to the exact symbol it changes. The flaw everything inherits

Strip the LLM case down and REINFORCE says: I know the answer was good, therefore every sampled decision along the way deserves credit. That is the assumption under attack from every direction at once. STAPO says some rare tokens are actively harmful. ASPO says tokens should not share directional dynamics merely because they share an outcome. GiGPO says steps need local credit. DAPO says long traces should not evaporate through response level normalization.

One caution on reading the graph. The arrows are conceptual lineage, not chronology or citation. GRPO and RLOO are partly a return toward REINFORCE, not machinery added on top of PPO, and VAPO is a live argument that deleting the critic was the wrong lesson to draw. Two of the boxes are called DAPO by different groups in different years. Treat the tree as a map of disagreements.
